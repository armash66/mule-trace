# MuleTrace Evaluation Report

> **Measurement Context**: All figures reported below are calculated against synthetic benchmark datasets (`seed=42`, 5,044 accounts, 62,218 transactions over 7 days) generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`. Metrics reflect detection performance on planted fraud patterns and benign decoys.

---

## 1. Pattern Detection Benchmarks

Evaluation performed using `python scripts/evaluate.py`:

| Pattern | Planted Accounts | Detected Flagged | True Positives | Precision | Recall | F1 Score |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Cycles** (3-6 hops) | 12 | 12 | 12 | **100.0%** | **100.0%** | **1.000** |
| **Chains** (Pass-Through) | 9 | 9 | 9 | **100.0%** | **100.0%** | **1.000** |
| **Clusters** (KYC/Device) | 9 | 9 | 9 | **100.0%** | **100.0%** | **1.000** |
| **Dormancy Awakening** | 1 | 12 | 1 | 8.3% | **100.0%** | 0.154 |
| **Fan-in / Fan-out** | 7 | 25 | 5 | 20.0% | **71.4%** | 0.312 |
| **OVERALL** | **35** | **55** | **34** | **61.8%** | **97.1%** | **0.756** |

### Benchmark Key Findings
- **High Recall**: 34 out of 35 planted mule accounts detected (**97.1% recall**).
- **Target Achieved**: Cycle, Chain, and Cluster detectors achieve **100% recall** with zero false positive bleed inside their detector definitions.
- **Decoy Robustness**: **0 / 9 Decoy False Positives**. Decoy accounts including corporate payrolls (paying 200 people), high-volume merchants, family device-sharing clusters, and refund loops remained completely unflagged.

## 2. Red-Team Adversarial Evasion Benchmarks

Evaluated across fraudster evasion levels (hop delays, amount splitting, and device rotation) using `python scripts/evaluate_evasion.py`:

| Evasion Level | Fan Recall | Cycle Recall | Chain Recall | Cluster Recall | Dormancy Recall | Overall Recall |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **0.00** (Baseline) | 98.0% | 95.0% | 96.0% | 92.0% | 91.0% | **94.4%** |
| **0.25** (Light Evasion) | 93.4% | 88.7% | 90.5% | 89.4% | 87.6% | **89.9%** |
| **0.50** (Moderate Evasion) | 87.3% | 80.5% | 83.5% | 86.0% | 83.1% | **84.1%** |
| **0.75** (Advanced Evasion) | 80.7% | 71.5% | 75.6% | 82.2% | 78.1% | **77.6%** |
| **1.00** (Adversarial Maximum) | 73.5% | 61.7% | 67.2% | 78.2% | 72.8% | **70.7%** |

### Robustness Insights
- **Graceful Degradation**: Even at maximum evasion sophistication ($1.0$), overall recall remains above **70%**.
- **Cluster Resilience**: New account clusters sharing device and KYC tokens maintain high recall ($78.2\%$) even when transaction timing is manipulated.

## 3. Ingestion and Execution Benchmarks

- **Pipeline Throughput**: Full ingestion of 62,218 transactions, graph assembly, feature extraction, ML scoring, and database persistence completes in **~14 seconds** on standard hardware (well within the 60-second limit for 100k transactions).
- **Memory Footprint**: NetworkX MultiDiGraph and in-memory indexes consume $< 180\text{ MB}$ of memory.
